Our Programs

Learning That Grows With Every Child

From the Early Years to Grade 7

Smart Start offers daycare and academy classes, led by educators who are committed to a solid foundation and a love for learning.

Babies, Grade R & Daycare

A warm, safe and loving start. Our babies class and Grade R focus on care, play, sensory learning, motor skills and the first steps of school readiness.

Grades 1–7

Academy classes that build literacy, numeracy and life skills. Subjects across the grades include English, IsiZulu, Mathematics, Life Skills, Social Sciences, Natural Sciences and Technology, Life Orientation, EMS and Creative Art.

Enrichment & School Life

Learning continues beyond the lesson. Graduation, leadership as prefects, sport and outings give scholars a chance to try new and exciting things.
