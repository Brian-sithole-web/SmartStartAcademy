Our Mission

A Love for Learning. A Foundation to Build On.

We aim to foster our scholars’ love for learning, encourage them to try new and exciting things, and give them a solid foundation to build on.

We aim to build Smart Start Academy into a leading organisation of education that will contribute to the development of the human capacity of South Africa.

Our vision is for each child to develop a curiosity for learning, discover their interests, and grow in their love of learning.

Leadership

Led by a Passion for Education

Mr M Njaph is School Director and Principal. He is a dedicated lifelong learner with 5 years of teaching experience and a Curriculum Studies Honours candidate at the University of Johannesburg.

He teaches Grade 7 Mathematics and EMS, and Grade 6 Mathematics and Life Skills. His mission is a safe, inclusive and motivating environment where every child can achieve their full potential.

A great school is built on strong values, dedicated educators and empowered learners. Together, we create a future of endless possibilities.
