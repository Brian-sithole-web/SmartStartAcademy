Our Approach

More Than Daycare. A Foundation for Life.

Philosophy

Every Child Deserves a Strong Foundation

We foster a love for learning, encourage scholars to try new things, and give them a solid foundation to build on — in the classroom and beyond it.

01

Early Childhood Education

We advocate for the early years. Babies, Grade R and the first grades are where curiosity, confidence and a love of learning take root.

02

Educational Outcomes

We work to improve educational outcomes, so every scholar makes real progress and is ready for the next step.

03

Motor Skills

We develop motor skills through play, movement and practical activity, from the babies class through the grades.

04

Inclusive Practices

We implement inclusive practices so classrooms are places where every learner feels they belong and can take part.

05

Parent Satisfaction

We want families to feel informed, respected and confident in the care and education their children receive.

06

Professional Development

We foster professional development for staff, because educators who keep learning raise the quality of teaching for every child.

In Practice

Learn. Explore. Discover.

Our vision is for each child to develop a curiosity for learning, discover their interests, and grow in their love of learning.

That is why the classroom, the campus and the world beyond the school gates all belong to the Smart Start experience — from daily lessons to graduation and community outings.
